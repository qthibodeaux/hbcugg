## The Havoc Chemistry / "Special Log" Concept
Inspired by high-fidelity game logs, this component maps the "butterfly effect" of a defensive play. 
- **Concept:** A "chemistry" flow that links different defensive actions. e.g., A "QB Hurry" by Player A leads directly to a "Tip/PD" by Player B, which leads to an "Interception" by Player C.
- **Visual:** A horizontal or vertical timeline/node flow where nodes are sized by EPA impact. 
- **Goal:** Shift the focus from "who got the stat" to "how the unit forced the outcome."
