The Alabama State Hornets are the college football team representing the Alabama State University. The Hornets play in NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) as a member of the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC).

Hornet Stadium serves as the facility for Alabama State football games and practices. The 120-yard field turf playing field provides training to ASU football in all weather conditions. In June 2011, construction on the Houston Markham Football Complex was completed. The two-story, 30,000-square-foot facility serves as the new home of the football program.

Rivalries

The Magic City Classic is the highest attended and most anticipated regular season ASU football game every year. The Hornets take on in-state rival Alabama A&M Bulldogs in Birmingham, Alabama.

The Turkey Day Classic is an annual event played yearly on Thanksgiving between ASU and another opponent, most recently Prairie View A&M in 2019 and has been played almost consecutively since 1924, except for 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic.

A new era in Alabama State University football began on Thanksgiving Day 2012 with the official opening of The New ASU Stadium, a 26,500- seat on-campus facility which now serves as the new home for Hornet Football. The $62 million multi-purpose facility has a synthetic turf playing surface and is among the best state-of-the-art stadiums in the country.

It is complete with 20 suites, loge and club seating and five locker rooms.
The stadium sits off right off of I-85 near the I-65 intersection near downtown Montgomery.

About Us

The Mighty Marching Hornets is a prestigious organization that has brought stature and honor to Alabama State University. Founded in 1964, the Mighty Marching Hornets Band has helped several generations of young men and women to become illustrious citizens. The Band counts with many alumni that have risen to the pinnacle of their professional endeavors.
IMG_6764.jpg
Mission

The rich and glorious history of the Mighty Marching Hornets Band makes it an organization of great honor and discipline. Each member and student is expected to honor this tradition by following the best of examples in academic excellence, behavior, discipline, respect, and musicianship. As such, each student pledges to serve as ambassadors of goodwill for Alabama State University - to our State and Nation - and to present themselves as a showcase for the University, lending an atmosphere of elegance, sophistication, esprit de corps, respect, and dignity to all who observe us.
Vision

The Mighty Marching Hornets aspire to be the best playing and marching unit and the most creative and exciting half-time show producers. Its ultimate goal is to become the most famous musical organization for college and university bands today.

4. Cultural Traditions

Tailgate Culture: "Vendor Valley" located directly in front of ASU Stadium; "Connection Yard Fest" begins pre-game; tailgating lots open early with food vendors, music, and community gatherings; the Turkey Day Classic draws 30,000+ and includes a pep rally, parade, and concert on Thanksgiving
Fight Song: "Hail Alabama"
Signature Chants: "Go Hornets!" / "Sting 'em!" — general crowd chants; Turkey Day Classic and Magic City Classic atmospheres are among the loudest in HBCU football

5. Historical Narrative
Brief History: The Alabama State Hornets are the college football team representing Alabama State University. The Hornets play in NCAA Division I Football Championship Subdivision (FCS) as a member of the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC). Hornet Stadium serves as the facility for Alabama State football games and practices. The 120-yard field turf playing field provides training to ASU football in all weather conditions. In June 2011, construction on the Houston Markham Football Complex was completed. The two-story, 30,000-square-foot facility serves as the new home of the football program.
Historic Milestones:
  - 1901: First football game in program history, played against Tuskegee
  - 1924: Turkey Day Classic tradition begins — now the nation's oldest Black college football classic
  - 1935 & 1939: SIAC Conference Championships
  - 1965 & 1966: Back-to-back SIAC Championships
  - 1982: ASU joins the Southwestern Athletic Conference (SWAC)
  - 1991: Undefeated season (11–0–1), SWAC Championship, and Black College National Championship under coach Houston Markham — the most dominant season in program history
  - 2004: SWAC Championship
  - 2012: New $62M ASU Stadium opens
  - 2019: Mighty Marching Hornets become first HBCU band to lead the Rose Bowl Parade
  - 2024: 100th Turkey Day Classic played; ASU wins 34–6
  - 2025: ASU wins 4th consecutive Magic City Classic 56–13 (longest streak since 1990–1995)

Fun Facts:
  - The Turkey Day Classic (vs. Tuskegee) is the nation's oldest Black college football classic, drawing over 30,000 fans each Thanksgiving
  - The Magic City Classic (vs. Alabama A&M) is the largest HBCU event in the nation, attracting nearly 70,000+ fans annually at Legion Field in Birmingham
  - Over 20 ASU alumni have played in the NFL, including linebacker Eddie Robinson (11-year NFL career), who was a key player on the legendary 1991 national championship team
  - The Mighty Marching Hornets appeared in Beyoncé's landmark 2019 Homecoming special on Netflix
  - ASU was founded in 1867 by nine formerly enslaved men — known as the "Marion Nine" — who raised $500 to start the school
  - Rosa Parks, one of the most iconic figures of the Civil Rights Movement, is among ASU's notable alumni connections
  - The 1991 Hornets went undefeated through 20 consecutive games across the 1990–1991 seasons — the last SWAC team to finish a season undefeated
