# SWAC D-line deep dive: tweet copy

2026 through Week 7. Games against SWAC/MEAC opponents only. Each tweet carries one of the three images from Team Analytics > Charts > Deep Dive, or no image at all.

---

**1 · Lead (no image)**
Is the SWAC D-line down this year? No. 2025 was the dip.

Sack rate by season: 7.1% in 2024, 4.8% in 2025, 6.8% now.

The run defense is what hasn't come back. Opponents run successfully 44.6% of the time and average 5.33 yards a carry, the most since 2019. 🧵

**2 · It's the second level (no image)**
When people say the front is down, they mostly mean the linebackers.

LB run TFLs: 1.56 a game in 2019, 0.75 now. The line makes 52% of the front's backfield plays, up from 41%.

**3 · Teams (image: Who got better up front)**
ASU's linemen went from 1.89 to 4.60 line plays a game (sacks, TFLs, forced fumbles, PBUs). 85% of 2025's line plays came back, under the same head coach (Eddie Robinson Jr.) and DC (Todd Middleton).

Grambling's 2025 linemen who graduated (Bryce Cage, Warren Robinson) made 74% of its line plays. With new line coaches, Horton's jump and transfers Hookfin, Exume and Green, it leads the SWAC at 6.00 a game, up from 3.88.

Southern changed its head coach, DC and line coach and dropped from 3.55 to 2.25.

**4 · Staff (rides with image 3)**
Teams that kept their head coach, DC and line coach added +0.9 line plays a game (sacks, TFLs, forced fumbles, PBUs). Teams with at least one new coach added +0.2. Twelve teams make that a pattern, not proof.

**5 · Situational (image: When it matters)**
Late and close is where it hurts. Runs work 47% of the time in the 4th quarter within one score, and defenses stop only 40% of 4th downs. Both are the worst since 2019.

Opponents scored a TD on 4 of 17 red-zone trips against ASU's defense, and 14 of 16 against Grambling's. Alcorn is 0 of 7 on short-yardage stops.

**6 · Pass rush vs pass D (no image)**
A pass rush isn't the same as a pass defense.

FAMU has 2 sacks, neither from a lineman, and still has the 2nd-best pass D per dropback.

Grambling sacks the QB on 8.5% of dropbacks and gives up the most per dropback. 26% of the completions against it go for 16+ yards.

**7 · Fading (no image)**
D-line sacks per game by week: 1.36, 1.05, 0.75, 0.70 (Weeks 4–7). 2024 had the same October dip and recovered in November. Worth watching, too early to call.

**8 · Players (image: Who's making plays)**
Jon Horton (GSU) has 4 sacks, 3.5 TFLs and a forced fumble in 4 games: 16 expected points taken away, the most in the league.

New faces producing: Jordan Thompson (TSU, from Kentucky State) with 3.5 sacks, Thad Parson (ASU) and Bryson Jennings (SU) with 10 run stops each.

Ckelby Givens (SU) had 8.5 sacks last year and has 1 so far.

**9 · Size (rides with the When it matters image)**
SWAC lines are heavier. The average listed D-lineman weighs 266 lb in 2026, up from 264 in 2019.

---

### Don't tweet
- **Pressure or hurry rates.** Home stat crews chart anywhere from 1 to 14 hurries per 100 dropbacks.
- **"Horton forced 2 turnovers."** One sack was a fumble, and Alcorn recovered it.
- **Clutch, short-yardage and red-zone percentages.** These are 5–47 plays per team, so use counts ("4 of 7").
